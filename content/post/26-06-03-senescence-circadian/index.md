**Frontiers in Immunology · Published 2026-06-03**

A four-gene score connects senescence and circadian programs with prognosis and immune heterogeneity in colorectal cancer.

The study combines public cohorts, single-cell analyses and experimental validation. Higher SCore was associated with poorer survival. NOX4 experiments support a redox and inflammatory component; broader immune and circadian interpretations remain hypothesis-generating.

**Qi Zhao's role:** Co-author, Sun Yat-sen University Cancer Center.
