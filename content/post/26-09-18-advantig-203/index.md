**Nature Communications · Published 2026-09-18**

A randomized phase II trial evaluates dual checkpoint blockade and exploratory immune biomarkers in advanced esophageal squamous cell carcinoma.

The 125-patient trial did not meet its primary objective-response endpoint. Adding ociperlimab to tislelizumab did not significantly improve efficacy. Exploratory analyses identified immune-inflamed signatures that warrant further investigation.

**Qi Zhao's role:** Co-author, Sun Yat-sen University Cancer Center.
