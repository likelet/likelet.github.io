**Nature Communications · Published 2026-08-05**

Single-cell profiling links naïve B cells and vitamin B5 metabolism to immune responses in HER2-positive gastric cancer.

Patient samples and cell experiments reveal changes in B–T cell interactions during immunochemotherapy. Vitamin B5 improved anti-PD-1 responses in mouse models; these findings do not establish a clinical benefit from supplementation in patients.
